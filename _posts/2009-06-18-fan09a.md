abstract: We demonstrate that a number of sociology models for social network dynamics
  can be viewed as continuous time Bayesian networks (CTBNs). A sampling-based approximate
  inference method for CTBNs can be used as the basis of an expectation-maximization
  procedure that achieves better accuracy in estimating the parameters of the model
  than the standard method of moments algorithmfromthe sociology literature. We extend
  the existing social network models to allow for indirect and asynchronous observations
  of the links. A Markov chain Monte Carlo sampling algorithm for this new model permits
  estimation and inference. We provide results on both a synthetic network (for verification)
  and real social network data.
