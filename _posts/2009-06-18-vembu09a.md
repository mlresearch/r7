abstract: We consider MAP estimators for structured prediction with exponential family
  models. In particular, we concentrate on the case that efficient algorithms for
  uniform sampling from the output space exist. We show that under this assumption
  (i) exact computation of the partition function remains a hard problem, and (ii)
  the partition function and the gradient of the log partition function can be approximated
  efficiently. Our main result is an approximation scheme for the partition function
  based on Markov Chain Monte Carlo theory. We also show that the efficient uniform
  sampling assumption holds in several application settings that are of importance
  in machine learning.
