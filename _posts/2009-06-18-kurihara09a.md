abstract: This paper studies quantum annealing (QA) for clustering, which can be seen
  as an extension of simulated annealing (SA). We derive a QA algorithm for clustering
  and propose an annealing schedule, which is crucial in practice. Experiments show
  the proposed QA algorithm finds better clustering assignments than SA. Furthermore,
  QA is as easy as SA to implement.
