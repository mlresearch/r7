abstract: We outline a representation for discrete multivariate distributions in terms
  of interventional potential functions that are globally normalized. This representation
  can be used to model the effects of interventions, and the independence properties
  encoded in this model can be represented as a directed graph that allows cycles.
  In addition to discussing inference and sampling with this representation, we give
  an exponential family parametrization that allows parameter estimation to be stated
  as a convex optimization problem; we also give a convex relaxation of the task of
  simultaneous parameter and structure learning using group l1-regularization. The
  model is evaluated on simulated data and intracellular flow cytometry data.
