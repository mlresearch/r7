abstract: This paper presents studies on a deterministic annealing algorithm based
  on quantum annealing for variational Bayes (QAVB) inference, which can be seen as
  an extension of the simulated annealing for variational Bayes (SAVB) inference.
  QAVB is as easy as SAVB to implement. Experiments revealed QAVB finds a better local
  optimum than SAVB in terms of the variational free energy in latent Dirichlet allocation
  (LDA).
